# Code of conduct

Treat colleagues with respect. Discuss the work directly and make room for questions, different levels of experience, and corrections. Give specific, constructive feedback and credit other people's work.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Respect requests to stop unwelcome behavior.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer, Jannis Leidel, through your established direct collaboration channel. Do not put sensitive reports in public issues or pull requests. The maintainer will review reports confidentially, hear the relevant people, and choose a response proportionate to the conduct. Responses may include an edit, a request to stop, or removal of repository access.

These expectations apply to this repository and its related collaboration. This personal repository does not speak for conda governance or another project's maintainers.
